Who we are

OffCampus Dubai is a small, Dubai based team helping students find off campus housing without the broker fees. We list verified rooms and flats near the city’s universities and colleges, and match students up with flatmates they’ll actually get along with.

Why students choose us

Built around three problems every student flat hunt runs into.

Finding a place near campus in Dubai usually means broker fees, awkward roommate gambles, and hidden commissions. We built OffCampus Dubai to remove all three.

Feature 01

Roommate matchmaker

Tell us how you live: sleep schedule, budget, tidiness, study habits, and we pair you with students actually worth sharing a kitchen with.

Feature 02

No brokerage

We work directly with landlords and verified listings, so there’s no middleman fee tacked onto your rent before you even move in.

Feature 03

No commission

What you agree with the landlord is what you pay. OffCampus Dubai doesn’t take a cut of your rent or your deposit, ever.
